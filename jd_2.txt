Job Title: UI/UX Designer
Job Overview

We are seeking a creative and detail-oriented UI/UX Designer to design intuitive, engaging, and visually compelling digital experiences. The ideal candidate will translate user needs and business requirements into innovative design solutions that enhance usability, accessibility, and overall product satisfaction.

You will collaborate closely with developers, product managers, and stakeholders to create seamless user journeys across web and mobile platforms.


Key Responsibilities

Conduct user research, interviews, and usability testing to understand user behavior and pain points

Create wireframes, prototypes, user flows, and high-fidelity mockups

Design responsive interfaces for web and mobile applications

Develop design systems, style guides, and UI component libraries

Collaborate with frontend developers to ensure accurate design implementation

Continuously iterate designs based on feedback, analytics, and user testing

Ensure designs comply with accessibility standards and usability best practices

Stay updated with industry trends, tools, and emerging design methodologies


Required Qualifications

Bachelor’s degree in Design, Computer Applications, Human-Computer Interaction, or related field

Proven experience in UI/UX design with a strong portfolio

Proficiency in tools such as Figma, Adobe XD, Sketch, or similar

Strong understanding of user-centered design principles

Knowledge of responsive design and basic frontend concepts (HTML/CSS preferred)

Excellent communication and presentation skills


Preferred Qualifications

Experience designing SaaS products or mobile applications

Familiarity with usability testing tools and analytics platforms

Understanding of interaction design and microinteractions

Experience working in Agile environments


Key Skills

Wireframing and Prototyping

User Research and Testing

Interaction Design

Visual Design

Information Architecture

Problem-Solving

Collaboration and Communication


What We Offer
